While you're here

Three habits that make a timetable stick

Spacing

Spread, don't cram

Three 45‑minute sessions across the week beat one three‑hour block. Spacing out revision on the same topic helps it stay in memory.

Focus

Protect the first ten minutes

Most sessions are lost in the first ten minutes to your phone. Put it in another room before the slot starts, not after.

Review

Re‑read nothing, recall everything

Close the book and try to write down what you remember before checking. Testing yourself beats re‑reading, every time.
