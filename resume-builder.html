How do I download it as a PDF? v
Click "Download PDF" once your resume is generated. Your browser's print dialog opens; choose "Save as PDF" as the destination and save.
Should I add a photo? v
It's optional. Some regions and industries expect a photo on a resume, others actively discourage it. If you're applying internationally or through a large company's ATS, leaving it off is usually the safer choice.
Can I edit after generating? v
Yes. Click "Edit" on the result screen to change anything and regenerate instantly.
Is the layout really ATS-friendly? v
The output uses a single-column structure, standard section headers, and plain text with no tables or text boxes, which is what most ATS parsing guides recommend.
