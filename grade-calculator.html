What is a Grade Calculator?

A grade calculator helps students determine their overall course grade by combining scores from assignments, quizzes, exams, projects and other assessments according to their weights.

How Does a Grade Calculator Work?

Each assessment contributes to the final grade according to its percentage weight. For example, if an exam is worth 30% of your course grade, its score contributes 30% of the final weighted grade.

Grade Calculator Formula

Weighted Grade = Σ (Score × Weight) ÷ 100

Example

Suppose you score 90% on an assignment worth 20%, 80% on an exam worth 30%, and 95% on a project worth 50%. The calculator combines each score with its corresponding weight to calculate the overall course grade.

Common Letter Grade Scale

  • A: 90–100%
  • B: 80–89%
  • C: 70–79%
  • D: 60–69%
  • F: Below 60%

Grade boundaries can vary between schools, colleges and universities, so always check your institution's grading policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my current grade?

Enter each completed assessment's score and its percentage weight. The calculator will combine the weighted scores to estimate your current grade.

Do all assessment weights need to equal 100%?

If you are calculating the final course grade, the assessment weights should normally total 100%. For partial-course calculations, the calculator can still show the weighted contribution of the entered assessments.

Can I use this calculator on mobile?

Yes. The calculator is designed to work on both desktop and mobile browsers.
